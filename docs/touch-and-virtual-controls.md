# Touch and virtual controls

## Virtual controls

Settings › Virtual controls.

An Xbox layout with coloured A, B, X and Y buttons.

- **Show virtual controls:** Auto shows them only when no supported controller is connected. Always show and Never show ignore the controller.
- **Size:** Small, Medium or Large.
- **Opacity:** from 20% to 100%.
- **Fixed sticks:** sticks normally start wherever your thumb lands in the lower half. Fixed sticks stay in one spot on each side instead.

## No controller connected

If a game starts with no controller connected, it waits and asks. Turn a controller on and the game starts straight away, or choose **Use virtual controls** or **Start without controls** for that session. This screen is skipped when **Show virtual controls** is set to Always show.

## Touch as a mouse

Settings › Virtual controls › **Touch as mouse**. On by default.

For launchers, logins and menus that want a pointer.

- **With a controller:** tap the picture to click exactly there, and drag to drag.
- **With virtual controls showing:** the top half of the screen works like a laptop trackpad. Drag to move the pointer, tap to click, and tap then drag to hold the button.
- **Either way:** press and hold, or tap with two fingers, to right-click. Drag with two fingers to scroll.
