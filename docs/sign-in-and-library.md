# Sign-in and library

## Signing in

Open iPlayNow and choose your GeForce NOW provider: NVIDIA, or a regional partner. Then sign in on NVIDIA's (or your partner's) own sign-in page. When it finishes, the page hands back to the app through a local address, which is why iOS asks for **Local Network** access the first time.

Your sign-in stays on the phone, in the iOS Keychain. **Settings › Account › Sign out** removes it, and needs a second press to confirm.

You need a GeForce NOW membership. Your membership decides the most GeForce NOW will stream (see [Picture](picture.md)).

## Your library

The library is a grid of covers for the games you own on stores you've linked to GeForce NOW (Steam, Epic, Xbox and others). Link stores in GeForce NOW itself, then pull to refresh. Games are tagged RTX and HDR where GeForce NOW supports them.

- **Search** finds a game by name.
- **Store filter** shows only one store's games.
- **Sort** by recently played or A to Z.

The library opens instantly from the last list it fetched, then refreshes.

## Playing a game

Tap a cover and you're in the queue. iPlayNow waits for a rig if needed, sets it up, then streams. There are no tutorials or setup screens.

Games you own on more than one store ask once which store to play. Press and hold the cover, then **Choose store**, to change it later.

## Per-game video settings

Press and hold a cover, then **Video settings**, to give that game its own resolution, frame rate, bitrate, codec and HDR. Turn on **Custom video settings** to use them. Display and controller settings are always shared between games.

## With a controller

A controller moves around the grid, and a bar along the bottom shows what each button does:

| Button  | What it does              |
| ------- | ------------------------- |
| A       | Play                      |
| Y       | The game's video settings |
| X       | Choose the store          |
| View    | Change the sort           |
| LB / RB | Step through the stores   |
| Menu    | Open Settings             |

Settings also work with a controller: up and down move, left and right change the selected setting, A toggles or opens it and B goes back.
