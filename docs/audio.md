# Audio

## Game audio

The game's sound plays through the phone's speaker, or headphones and AirPods when they're connected.

## Microphone

Settings › Audio › **Microphone**. Off by default, and it applies from the next stream.

Talk in game and party chat. The first time, iOS asks to let iPlayNow use the microphone. If you said no, turn it on in the iOS Settings app under **Apps › iPlayNow**.

- **Mute:** while streaming, tap the top-left corner, then the mic button, or use the stream menu. A small red mic stays in the corner while you're muted.
- **Echo:** on the phone's speaker, echo cancellation keeps the game out of your mic, so your party doesn't hear it.
