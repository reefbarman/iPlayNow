# Controllers

## Supported controllers

Xbox, PlayStation (DualSense and DualShock 4), Backbone and other MFi controllers. With a DualSense or DualShock 4 connected, the app names buttons the PlayStation way (Cross, Circle, L2 and so on).

Hold **View + Menu** for a second during a stream to open the stream menu.

## The Xbox button and the Home button

The Xbox button opens GeForce NOW's in-game menu. To press it without a controller, tap the top-left corner of the stream, then the Xbox logo.

On iOS 27, iOS opens its Game Overlay when you press a controller's Home button, unless you let apps use it. The first time you play with a controller while iOS keeps Home for itself, the app offers to open that setting. **Settings › Controller › Home Button Settings** opens it any time. Once apps can use it, Home opens GeForce NOW's in-game menu.

## Rumble

Settings › Controller › Vibration.

- **Rumble:** turns all vibration on or off. Games rumble the controller, or the phone when the controller has no motors.
- **Controller vibration:** how strongly a controller's motors play rumble: Light, Medium or Full (the default).
- **Rumble updates:** how many vibration changes per second go to a wireless controller, from 10 to Unlimited (default 25). They share the controller's Bluetooth connection with your button presses, so too many can make the controls stop responding for a moment during heavy vibration. Lower it if that happens, raise it if vibration feels less detailed.
- **Phone haptics:** how strongly the phone itself vibrates, which it does with virtual controls or a controller without motors.

## Gyro aiming

Settings › Controller › Gyro aiming.

Turns the controller's motion (or the phone's, if the controller has none) into right stick movement, on top of the stick itself. Use it always, or only while holding LT or RB.

- **Look left/right by:** tilt like a steering wheel, or turn left and right.
- **Up/down speed:** makes looking up and down faster than left and right, as most games turn slower vertically.
- **Invert gyro up/down.**
- **Game dead zone:** how much stick movement the game ignores. Raise it until small, slow movements move the camera, and lower it if the camera drifts.
- **Dead zone per direction:** pushes left/right and up/down past the dead zone separately.

## Controls Guide

**Settings › Controller › Controls Guide** explains the controller shortcuts, touch as a mouse, the virtual controls and the hidden corner buttons on the stream.

## Controller Test

**Settings › Controller › Controller Test**, or from the stream menu.

A 3D controller (a DualSense for PlayStation controllers, an Xbox controller for anything else) lights up each button, stick and trigger as GeForce NOW gets it, turns with the controller's gyro, and tries rumble and haptics. A DualSense also gets touchpad, lightbar, player light and adaptive trigger tests, and an Xbox controller its trigger motors. Drag the controller to turn it, and hold **View + Menu** to close.
